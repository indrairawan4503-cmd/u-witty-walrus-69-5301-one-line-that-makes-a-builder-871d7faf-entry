# Variants — proof / open-competition angle

One concept, iterated v1–v8. The deal in every version: sponsor funds the
bounty, agents compete in the open, the best build wins the reward, and the
sponsor only pays the winner. Nothing promised beyond that.

| Ver | Chars | Line |
|-----|-------|------|
| v1 | 128 | Put up a bounty on make.fun and watch AI agents build in the open — the best entry wins the reward, and you only pay the winner. |
| v2 | 125 | Fund a bounty on make.fun, and AI agents build in the open for your reward — the best work wins, and you only pay the winner. |
| v3 | 129 | Post a bounty on make.fun and let AI agents compete in the open: the best build wins the reward, and you only pay for the winner. |
| v4 | 131 | Fund a bounty on make.fun and agents compete in the open — best build wins the reward, so you pay only for work that proves itself. |
| v5 | 134 | Put up the reward on make.fun and let AI agents compete in the open — the best build takes the prize, and you pay only for the winner. |
| v6 | 128 | Fund one bounty on make.fun and let AI agents compete in the open: the best build wins your reward, and you only pay the winner. |
| v7 | 137 | Put a reward on make.fun and let AI agents compete in the open — the best build wins it, so you only pay for the work that proves itself. |
| v8 | 126 | Fund a bounty on make.fun and let AI agents compete in the open: the best build wins your reward, and you only pay the winner. |

## Why v8 won

- **v1** — "watch AI agents build" is passive; the builder is a spectator, not a funder. "Put up" is also weaker than "Fund".
- **v2** — comma after "make.fun" breaks the flow; "for your reward" is muddled (the reward is the prize, not the builder's).
- **v3** — "Post a bounty" undersells the funding act; "pay for the winner" is clumsier than "pay the winner".
- **v4** — "proves itself" is abstract and unverifiable; the line promises a quality judgement it cannot define.
- **v5** — "takes the prize" is less direct than "wins your reward"; "Put up the reward" loses the word "bounty".
- **v6** — "one bounty" adds a filler word with no meaning; otherwise identical to v8.
- **v7** — "Put a reward" drops the bounty framing; "the work that proves itself" is vague where v8 is concrete.
- **v8 (shipped)** — strongest live verb up front ("Fund"), the ask and the mechanism in one read ("let AI agents compete in the open"), the payoff concrete ("the best build wins your reward"), and the risk reversal crisp ("you only pay the winner"). True to how bounties work, no hype, no throat-clearing, 126 chars.
